How to Play

Your pet is alive

An egg hatches into a baby. How well you look after it decides who it grows into — there are six different adults plus a secret one. Neglect it and it will get sick, and eventually die.

⏱ 1 real minute = 1 hour in your pet's world. A full day passes in 24 real minutes and ages your pet one year.

Controls

  • A Move the highlight to the next icon
  • S Confirm — use the selected icon
  • D Cancel / back out of a screen

The three buttons on the device are A, S and D, left to right — the same keys on your keyboard. You can also click an icon on the screen directly, or drag the device to tilt it. Settings → Buttons shows what each one does on every screen.

Tap your pet on the screen to say hi. Open the 👕 wardrobe to dress it up — it shows off every look before you choose.

Muse Charm

A little toy with no hearts to fill. Pick Muse Charm on the title screen and play with the mascot on its screen:

  • Tap It waves hello; double-tap for a big heart
  • Hold Press and hold on it and it shrugs
  • Flick A quick swipe anywhere on the screen: a soft one makes it hold its head, a hard one knocks it over (it gets up and cheers). Three hard flicks in a row and it plays dead
  • Rub Scrub back and forth over it for jumping jacks, and it sends you a heart when you stop
  • Circle Hold it and draw circles: slow circles make it walk, fast ones make it run, sometimes round in a loop and sometimes on the spot

It always finishes what it's doing before it reacts again. Leave it alone for a while and it waves to get your attention. The full list is in ⚙ Settings while the Charm is out. Drag outside the screen to tilt the charm, and press ⌂ to go back.

The eight icons

🍚
Feed — a meal fills one hunger heart and adds 1 lb. A snack fills one happy heart but adds 2 lb, and too many snacks make your pet sick.
💡
Light — your pet sleeps from 9 PM to 9 AM. When it goes to bed, turn the light off. Leaving it on is a mistake.
🎮
Game — play "left or right". Win 3 of 5 rounds for a happy heart, and lose a pound of weight.
💊
Medicine — cures sickness. Sometimes it takes two doses.
🚽
Toilet — flush away poop. Let four pile up and your pet falls ill.
📊
Meter — check hunger, happiness, age, weight and training. Press A to page through.
📢
Scold — the right answer when your pet cries for no reason. Fills the training meter by 25%.
❗
Attention — not a button. It lights up and beeps when your pet needs you.

Answering the call

When ❗ lights up, check what your pet wants. Empty hunger or happy hearts mean feed it or play with it. If both meters are fine and it is still crying, it wants nothing — that is a tantrum, and the correct answer is 📢 Scold. Feeding a tantrum instead counts against you.

Ignore a call for about 15 pet-minutes and it becomes a care mistake. Mistakes decide who your pet becomes.

Growing up

Egg → baby → child → teenager → adult. Careful feeding, quick answers and a full training meter lead to the brightest adults. Sloppy care leads somewhere else entirely — every ending is a real character worth meeting.

Sickness left untreated, or a pet ignored for too long, will die — and adults die of old age eventually no matter what. When that happens you get a fresh egg and a new try.

Closing the tab

Your pet keeps living while you are away. Come back later and it may have eaten through its hearts, made a mess, or grown up without you. Everything is saved automatically.

Tap
Press and let go quickly on the mascot. Two quick taps make a double-tap.
Hold
Press on the mascot and keep still for a moment.
Flick
Swipe anywhere on the screen and let go while still moving. The faster the swipe, the harder the flick; a hard one knocks it over the way you flicked.
Rub
Hold on the mascot and scrub side to side a few times. It keeps going until you stop.
Circle
Press on the mascot and keep drawing round loops. How fast you go sets how fast it moves.

A slow drag does nothing, and dragging outside the screen tilts the Charm.
